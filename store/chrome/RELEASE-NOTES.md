Initial iShield Extension release for Chrome.

- Cosmetic page cleanup and packaged filter scriptlets
- Phishing, scam, and malware protection
- Fingerprint-protection controls
- YouTube and Facebook cleanup
- Local allowlist, Strict controls, protection activity, and statistics
- Signed filter-list updates
- No account, analytics, telemetry, or browsing-data upload
