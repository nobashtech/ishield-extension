## AdGuard content engines

Bundled executable libraries: AdGuard TSUrlFilter 6.0.3 (https://github.com/AdguardTeam/tsurlfilter), ExtendedCss 2.2.0 (https://github.com/AdguardTeam/ExtendedCss), and Scriptlets 2.5.1 (https://github.com/AdguardTeam/Scriptlets), under GPL-3.0. They provide filter matching, procedural element hiding and statically compiled page scriptlets.
The Base and Tracking Protection content lists come from https://github.com/AdguardTeam/FiltersRegistry under GPL-3.0. Exact commits, source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `rules/adguard/ATTRIBUTION.json`. Executable rules are compiled at build time; the installed extension does not fetch executable filtering code.
The distributed AdGuard integration bundles are included in the browser source trees with their attribution metadata. Before a public release, the corresponding pinned upstream/build-source package used to reproduce these generated bundles must be included with the release source archive or otherwise provided in a GPL-compliant way. The generated browser packages themselves do not fetch executable filtering code.

## Redirect resources

The neutral stand-in files under `web-accessible-resources/redirects/` are generated by `@adguard/tswebextension` from `@adguard/scriptlets` (both GPL-3.0-only, https://github.com/AdguardTeam/tsurlfilter and https://github.com/AdguardTeam/Scriptlets).
